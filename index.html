PRESERVATION SCIENCE

Food Preservation Technologies

Technologies used to slow deterioration and maintain food quality.

❄️

Refrigeration

Reduces the rate of microbial growth and slows many quality changes.

MEDIUM EFFECT
🧊

Freezing

Greatly slows microbial and enzymatic activity during storage.

VERY HIGH EFFECT
💨

Drying

Reduces available moisture and improves stability of many foods.

📦

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Food Science Meets Smart Technology

SHELFSAFE is an educational food technology platform designed to demonstrate how food deterioration, storage conditions and preservation technologies can be connected through a digital interface.

The Smart Advisor uses a rule-based educational model to evaluate entered conditions. Its results should be treated as learning guidance rather than laboratory-validated shelf-life predictions.
